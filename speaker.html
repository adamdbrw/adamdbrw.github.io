Long (about 250 words)

Adam Dąbrowski is CTO of Robotec.ai in Warsaw. He works on the engineering that takes foundation models from demos to dependable operation on real robots, the theme of his 2026 Davos Tech Summit keynote, The Blueprint for Physical AI. He was among the first five people at Robotec.ai and has led its technology through its bootstrapped growth to 50 people.

He has written software since 2005. Before robotics he developed clients for Gadu-Gadu, then Poland's most-used instant messenger, worked as a system analyst on integrating information systems at PZU, Poland's largest insurer, and worked on a risk-rating system for Poland's largest bank at Deloitte. In 2014 he moved to robotics at PIAP, the Industrial Research Institute for Automation and Measurements. There he led the Autonomy group and PIAP's part in two EU projects: OFERA, which produced micro-ROS (ROS 2 for microcontrollers), and R5-COP, a 30-partner project on modular, ROS-based robots, whose exhibit won the ARTEMIS Exhibition Award in 2015.

At Robotec.ai since 2017 he has been technical lead, principal engineer, VP Robotics & Simulation and, since 2024, CTO. He co-created ROS 2 for Unity, which ships inside AWSIM, an open-source driving simulator for Autoware, started the ROS 2 integration of Open 3D Engine, improved rosbag2 recording performance, and conceived RAI, an open-source agent framework for embodied AI. He proposed the ROS Simulation Interfaces, implemented in Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and O3DE, and is lead author of REP 158, OpenUSD conventions for robot simulation assets. In 2026 he conceived, designed and wrote most of Slingshot, Robotec.ai's product that turns a site scan into a simulation-ready digital twin.

He chaired O3DE SIG-Simulation from 2023, leads the ROS Embodied AI Community Group he founded in 2024, has been a member of the Open Source Robotics Alliance's Physical AI SIG since its start, leads the Simulations & Standards working group of the European research network COST API-Net, and has been an Independent Expert Evaluator for the European Commission since 2018. He holds an MSc in Information Technology from the University of Warsaw.

Talk topics

  1. The Blueprint for Physical AI: engineering to deploy foundation models. Why demos at 90% are not industry at 99.9%, and the four steps that close the gap. Keynote, executive audience.
  2. Simulation as the test bench for robot operations. OpenUSD, REP 158 and the ROS Simulation Interfaces: one interface across simulators, and assets that move between them. Technical.
  3. Agents on robots. Modular skills and zero-trust safety, “safe even with the dumbest AI in charge”, with the open-source RAI framework. Technical.
  4. How an idea becomes a robotics standard. From a proposal to implementations by competing simulators: what it takes, and what to hand over. Community and open source.

Formats: keynote, talk, panel, hands-on workshop.
