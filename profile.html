Adam Dąbrowski

CTO, Robotec.ai · Warsaw, Poland

adamdbrw.github.io · linkedin.com/in/dabrowskiadam · github.com/adamdbrw

Summary

Engineering leader for robot simulation and physical AI; in software since 2005 and in robotics since 2014. He proposed the ROS Simulation Interfaces, implemented in Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and O3DE, and is lead author of REP 158, OpenUSD conventions for robot simulation assets, accepted by the ROS and Gazebo project management committees. He started or shaped open-source robotics software in use today: the robotics stack of Open 3D Engine, ROS 2 for Unity (inside AWSIM), rosbag2 recording performance and the RAI agent framework, and was first author of the founding requirements of micro-ROS. He was elected Chair of O3DE SIG-Simulation in 2023, coordinates a working group of the OSRA Physical AI SIG, leads a working group of a European research network, and has evaluated research proposals for the European Commission since 2018.

Standards and open source

  • REP 158 (2026): OpenUSD conventions for robot simulation assets. Lead author, with co-authors from Robotec.ai, NVIDIA and Ekumen; core schema. Accepted by the ROS and Gazebo project management committees, September 2026.
  • ROS Simulation Interfaces (2024): proposed the standard and wrote the first version; developed with NVIDIA contributors and reviews from Gazebo maintainers; listed maintainer on ROS Index. Implemented in Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and O3DE.
  • RAI (2024): open-source agent framework for embodied AI. Conceived and directed; co-author of the PAAMS 2025 paper. Cited in later research.
  • O3DE ROS 2 Gem (2022): RFC, first code, top committer of the original repository. The official O3DE robotics stack, maintained by O3DE SIG-Simulation.
  • ROS 2 for Unity and ros2cs (2020–2021): co-creator; first commit in both, top committer of ROS 2 for Unity. Ships inside AWSIM, an open-source driving simulator for Autoware started by TIER IV.
  • rosbag2 (2020–2021): benchmarks and report, optimized SQLite defaults, --regex and --exclude. Shipped in ROS 2.
  • micro-ROS (2018): first author of the founding scenarios and requirements (OFERA). micro-ROS is now a Zephyr Project module.
