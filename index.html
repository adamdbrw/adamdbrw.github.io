Adam Dąbrowski

CTO of Robotec.ai in Warsaw. Since 2018 his work has been taken up well beyond his own company: the micro-ROS requirements (2018), the optimised SQLite recording defaults in ROS 2's rosbag2 (2021), and two standards for robot simulation: the ROS Simulation Interfaces he proposed, now shipped by Gazebo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim and declared as a dependency in more than 60 public GitHub repositories, and REP 158, the ROS and Gazebo standard for robot simulation assets in OpenUSD, of which he is lead author. He still builds: in 2026 he conceived, designed and wrote most of Slingshot, Robotec.ai's product that turns a site scan into a simulation-ready digital twin.

Dąbrowski is pronounced dom-BROF-skee; also written Dabrowski. Every claim on this site is listed with its sources and marked verified or self-reported: see Sources.

Point of view

Physical AI will be won by engineering, not only by bigger models: safety that holds “even with the dumbest AI in charge”, simulation that tests operations at scale before anything touches the floor, modular robot skills instead of monoliths, and small, additive wins first.

AI doesn't have to do everything to be useful.
From The Blueprint for Physical AI, his keynote in the main-stage session Foundation Models in Robotics, Davos Tech Summit, July 2026 (slides, PDF)

Career

2024 – now
CTO, Robotec.ai, Warsaw. Technology strategy and engineering across robot simulation, digital twins, physical AI and open source. He was among the first five people at Robotec.ai and has led its technology through its bootstrapped growth to 50 people. He mentors its engineers in agentic, AI-agent-driven software development.
2017 – 2024
Robotec.ai: Technical Lead (2017), Principal Software Engineer (2021), VP Robotics & Simulation (2023), leading a department of six teams.
2014 – 2019
PIAP, Industrial Research Institute for Automation and Measurements (2017–2019 half-time, alongside Robotec.ai). Led the Autonomy group (11 people, robotics and deep learning) and PIAP's part in two EU projects: OFERA, which produced micro-ROS, and R5-COP (30 partners, modular ROS-based robots), whose exhibit won the ARTEMIS Exhibition Award in 2015.
2005 – 2014
Software engineer and system analyst: the C++/Qt client of Gadu-Gadu, then Poland's most-used instant messenger; system analyst on integrating information systems at PZU, Poland's largest insurer; digital TV decoding; a risk-rating system for Poland's largest bank at Deloitte.
Since 2018
Independent Expert Evaluator, European Commission (Horizon 2020, Horizon Europe): architecture, feasibility and execution risk of research proposals.
Education
MSc in Information Technology, University of Warsaw. Polish (native), English, German.
