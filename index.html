Adam Dąbrowski

I am CTO of Robotec.ai in Warsaw, working on open standards for robot simulation and the products built on them. I proposed the ROS Simulation Interfaces, now implemented in Gazebo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and I am lead author of REP 158, OpenUSD conventions for robot simulation assets, accepted by the ROS and Gazebo project management committees in 2026. I also designed Slingshot, Robotec.ai's product that turns a site scan into a simulation-ready digital twin, and wrote most of its code.

Point of view

Physical AI will be won by engineering, not only by bigger models: safety that holds “even with the dumbest AI in charge”, simulation that tests operations at scale before anything touches the floor, modular robot skills instead of monoliths, and small, additive wins first.

Today's demos deliver about 90%. The industry runs at 99.9%.
From The Blueprint for Physical AI, my keynote in the main-stage session Foundation Models in Robotics, Davos Tech Summit, July 2026 (slides, PDF)
