Bio

Adam Dąbrowski (also written Dabrowski) is CTO of Robotec.ai in Warsaw. His work is the engineering that takes foundation models from impressive demos to dependable operation on real robots. That was the theme of his 2026 Davos Tech Summit talk, The Blueprint for Physical AI.

For more than a decade Adam has built the layers that let robotics tools work together, then handed them to the community as standards. At PIAP, the Industrial Research Institute for Automation and Measurements, he evaluated the resilience of ROS 2 communication (ROSCon 2016) and researched human–machine interfaces for unmanned and construction robots. He was also first author of the founding scenarios and requirements of micro-ROS in the EU-funded OFERA project. At Robotec.ai he led performance work on rosbag2, the ROS 2 data recorder, and co-created ROS 2 for Unity, which now runs inside Autoware's AWSIM simulator. He started and led the ROS 2 integration of Open 3D Engine and conceived RAI, an open-source agent framework for embodied AI.

He proposed the ROS Simulation Interfaces, now implemented in Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and O3DE, and authored REP 158, the accepted ROS standard for OpenUSD simulation assets. He founded the ROS Embodied AI Community Group, now the Embodied Agentic AI working group of the OSRA Physical AI SIG, which he coordinates. He leads the Simulations & Standards working group of COST Action API-Net and is an independent expert evaluator for the European Commission. Adam studied at the University of Warsaw. He has spoken at ROSCon since 2016 and co-led a ROSCon 2026 workshop with AMD.

The Blueprint for Physical AI

Today's demos deliver 90%; industry runs at 99.9%. The missing 10% is a stopped supply chain, a broken machine, an injured worker. Closing that gap takes engineering, not just more data and training.

  1. Compute, inference-ready. ROS 2 communication resilience (2016), micro-ROS on microcontrollers (2018), the cloud-to-robot workshop with AMD (2026).
  2. Simulation, covering the whole cycle: train, prototype, then test operations at scale with metrics. ROS 2 in Unity and O3DE, the ROS Simulation Interfaces, REP 158 for OpenUSD.
  3. Robot skills, a modular framework, not a monolith. RAI: agents, connectors and tools on ROS 2. Safety assumes zero trust: “safe even with the dumbest AI in charge”.
  4. Adoption, small wins first. Inspection, debugging, getting a stuck robot unstuck: AI that adds value without replacing core processes.
