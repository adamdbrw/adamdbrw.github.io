Adam Dąbrowski

CTO of Robotec.ai in Warsaw. He leads engineering for robot simulation and physical AI. He proposed the ROS Simulation Interfaces, one ROS 2 interface now implemented in Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and O3DE, and is lead author of REP 158, the standard accepted by the ROS and Gazebo project management committees for describing robot simulation assets in OpenUSD, so they can move between simulators.

Dąbrowski is pronounced dom-BROF-skee; also written Dabrowski. Every claim on this site is listed with its sources and marked verified or self-reported: see Sources.

Point of view

Physical AI will be won by engineering, not only by bigger models: safety that holds “even with the dumbest AI in charge”, simulation that tests operations at scale before anything touches the floor, modular robot skills instead of monoliths, and small, additive wins first.

AI doesn't have to do everything to be useful.
From The Blueprint for Physical AI, his main-stage keynote at the Davos Tech Summit, July 2026 (slides, PDF)
